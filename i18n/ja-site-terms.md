# Site term sheet - Japanese (ja-JP)

Scope: buildwithjesus.com (site copy, not the guide).
Base: house locks (the guide's Japanese edition voice); review pending.

## Voice

- Register: soft です・ます調 (やわらかいです・ます調). Warm, plain, short sentences.
- Address the reader without pronouns. The words ティーン / ティーンエイジャー / 10代 /
  あなた / きみ / おまえ / お前 are BANNED (hard check at assembly); スマートフォン too
  (write スマホ).
- Full-width punctuation: 。、！？ for Japanese prose. No em dashes or en dashes.
- Western digits; entities stay entities.

and the WEB label.

  switcher and hreflang on every page.

## Prices and dates

- "Founding price" = 発売記念価格; "coming soon" = 近日公開.
- Amounts keep the English shape with (AUD): "$19.99 (AUD)", "$29.99 (AUD)", "$69.99 (AUD)",
  "$1,000 (AUD)" - all on the machinery keep list, so bare price runs stay identical.
- Dates: "2026年12月1日"; "until" = まで; "then" = その後.
  Shape: "発売記念価格 $19.99 (AUD)、2026年12月1日まで。その後 $29.99 (AUD)。"
