For groups and churches

Bring it to your group.

Two ways to run Build with Jesus with your young people: Brendan comes to you for a live build day, or you lead it yourself with the leaders guide. Both are made for churches, religious schools, homeschool families and charities.

In person

A build day, in your building.

Brendan travels to run the workshop live with your young people: a full day, real builds, everything agreed up front in writing. One host church for the groups around it, sharing the day and the cost. Around $1,000 a day (AUD), plus travel and expenses.

Lead it yourself

The leaders guide, at your pace.

Run the programme yourself, whenever suits your group. The guide walks you through it week by week: what to run, what to watch for, and how to keep it moving when term gets busy. $19.99 (AUD) as a founding price until 1 December 2026, then $29.99 (AUD), one purchase for every leader and every group, forever.
