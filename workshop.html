In person, anywhere in the world

Bring the workshop to your church.

In 2027, Brendan O'Keefe plans to travel to you and run the Build with Jesus workshop in person, in your building: a full day with your young people, working on real things for your church and community.

One day, in your building.

The workshop is the programme, live: the same three moves and the same real builds, run in person with your young people, your leaders, and the agent alongside.

In your building.

Your hall, your youth room, your wifi. The workshop comes to where your young people already are, rather than asking them to travel.

One host, many groups.

One church or school hosts the day for the groups around it: churches, schools, homeschool families, charities. The room grows, the day gets better, and the cost is shared.

Built for your church.

The day works on a real need your church has. The young people build together on their own machines, and hand over something finished.

Leaders leave with the plan.

A leaders session in the day: how to run the term, what to watch for, and how the agent helps without taking over.

The shape of the day.

A full day, from first passage to hand-over.

Before the day. Everyone arrives set up. Each young person buys the agent beforehand ($33.33, theirs to keep) and our onboarding tutorial walks them through installing it at home, a parent or leader alongside.

Morning: the moves, live. Learn, Build and Give, taught in the room. The young people meet the rhythm with their own hands, not a handout.

Through the day: one real build. A project for your church, built together on their own machines, with the agent teaching beside each of them.

The Bible, handled together. How the agent teaches a passage, where it stops, and why every good question goes back to your leaders.

Close: the hand-over. The finished thing is presented to the church, and your leaders take the plan for carrying the term forward.

What it costs.

Simple, and agreed up front before anything is booked.

Around $1,000 a day (AUD), plus travel and expenses.

The host books the day, and the groups around it share the cost between them. Travel, dates and the shape of the day are settled with you first, in writing.

Shared between groups. Three or four neighbouring churches or schools splitting the day pay near $300 each. Across forty young people, that works out near $25 each, less than the agent itself.

Extra days, same trip. Further days in the same region run at $850 a day.

Travel and expenses. The host books flights and simple accommodation directly, at cost. Nothing is added on top.

What the host church provides.

• A room with tables and power for a room full of laptops.

• Wifi that can carry the group.

• A screen or projector.

• A few of your own leaders in the room for the day.

We bring everything else: the plan, the agent, the builds, and the working material for the day.

A note on safeguarding. The day runs under your church’s safeguarding policy. Your leaders stay in the room and in charge throughout, young people are never left one-to-one with the facilitator, all the work happens in the open, and nothing is published anywhere before an adult leader has reviewed it.
