Safety and data

The plain version.

For parents, leaders and church boards: what stays private, what this site collects, and who is in charge.

What stays where it is

Every piece of work a young person makes stays on their own machine. It is not uploaded to us, and nothing is published anywhere without an adult leader reviewing it first.

A church’s data stays with the church. The programme runs on the young person’s computer, for the local group, and nothing is pooled into a shared platform.

The agent beside them

The agent is a reading companion, never an authority. It points back to your church and its leaders for teaching, and it is built so the leader stays the teacher.

Scripture is quoted exactly from the translations we ship, and each quote says which one. The agent never invents a verse, and a person reviews every line of faith content before it ships.

What this website collects

There are two forms: the subscribe box and the workshop register. We keep what you type there (your name, email and church or organisation) to reply to you, and nothing else.

Visits are counted with a small visit counter that uses no cookies and does not build a profile of you. There are no accounts, no tracking pixels and no advertising on this site.

Young people and leaders

The programme runs with the leaders in the room, under the host church’s safeguarding policy. Young people are never left one-to-one with an adult facilitator, all the work happens in the open, and every build is reviewed by a leader before it goes anywhere near the public internet.

Any questions, ask us directly.

Write to go@getonetoeight.org and a person will answer.
