Privacy: this website

There are two forms on this site: the subscribe box and the workshop register. We keep what you type there (your name, email and church or organisation) to reply to you, and nothing else.

Visits are counted with a small visit counter that uses no cookies and does not build a profile of you. There are no accounts, no tracking pixels and no advertising on this site.

If you email us or join a list, we use your address to talk to you about the programme. Every message tells you how to leave the list, and leaving takes one click.

Privacy: the agent and your messages

Lessons, builds and files live on the young person’s own machine and are not uploaded to us. When they message the agent, that message goes to the AI model chosen in their own OpenRouter account, and daily messages travel over Telegram or WhatsApp like any chat message.

OpenRouter, the model providers, Telegram and WhatsApp run their own data policies; the How we use AI and Safety and data pages set out the detail, including OpenRouter’s zero data retention documentation.

What reaches us: nothing automatically. We see what you send by email or a form, and nothing else.

Young people and parents

If the young person is under 18, an adult owns the setup: the paying OpenRouter account is created by a parent or guardian, and the messaging account is set up with their knowledge.

The agent messages one-to-one, never in group chats, and a parent can ask to read every message at any time. In workshops and groups, the programme runs with leaders in the room under the host church’s safeguarding policy.
